How MindCadence helps

Three ways we walk alongside you

Understand disease with trustworthy science

Clear, accurate explanations of the brain and neurological illness — complex science, in plain language.

Support families and caregivers

About MindCadence

Science should be accurate, understandable, and kind.

MindCadence was founded by a neuroscientist and long-time writer who believes understanding shouldn't require a medical degree. Our mission is to help people better understand the brain, illness, caregiving — and life.

"Every brain belongs to a person, and every person deserves to be understood."
